What we do, and what we don't

We are your parent's companion and advocate, not their doctor. Being clear about this keeps everyone safe.

✓ We do
  • Accompany your parent through the entire visit
  • Ask your questions and take detailed notes
  • Translate and explain medical terms simply
  • Document what the doctor said, medicines and next steps
  • Our service starts at the hospital and ends at the hospital
  • Keep you connected with the updtes. Be available for a call or WhatsApp

✗ We don't
  • Diagnose or give medical advice
  • Change, skip or suggest medicine doses
  • Make treatment decisions on your family's behalf
  • Handle your parent's cash, ATM cards or documents
  • Pay hospital bills. You pay the hospital directly.
  • House pickup and drop
  • Nursing care

Second opinions are given by licensed partner doctors, never by withyouseniors staff.

Frequently asked questions

Can I join the consultation live from abroad?

Yes. With the doctor's permission, we connect you by WhatsApp video or voice call during the consultation, so you can hear the doctor and ask questions yourself. If live joining isn't possible, we relay your questions and call you right after.

What if my parent has dementia, hearing loss or mobility issues?

Tell us during the pre-visit call. Companions are trained to speak slowly and clearly, watch for symptoms your parent may not mention, and arrange a wheelchair or assisted transport where needed. We note our observations in the summary.

What if the hospital doesn't allow a third person in the room?

Some hospitals limit who can enter consultation rooms. We carry your parent's written consent and your family's authorization. If entry is still refused, we brief your parent beforehand with the question list and speak with the doctor or nurse afterwards.

Who makes decisions in an emergency?

Your family's nominated decision-maker. Our companion alerts hospital staff and emergency services right away, then calls you. We never authorize admission or treatment ourselves.

How do you protect my parent's health data?

Reports and summaries are shared only with the family members you name. We don't record anything without consent, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

Do you give medical advice?

No. We are companions and advocates. We ask questions, take notes and explain what the doctor said in simple words. For a second opinion, we connect you with a licensed partner doctor.

Which cities and hospitals do you cover?

We currently serve Bangalore, with a focus on major multi-speciality hospitals. Message us about other cities. We are expanding.
