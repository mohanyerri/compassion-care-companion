Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Withyouseniors ("we", "us") accompanies seniors in India to hospital visits on behalf of their families. To do this, we handle personal and health information. We treat that information with care and handle it in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. What we collect

  • Contact details of the family members who book, and of the senior: names, phone numbers, email and address.
  • Health information you choose to share: medical history, current medicines, previous reports and prescriptions.
  • Visit information we create: notes from consultations, visit summaries, prescriptions and receipts.
  • Recordings, only if the senior and the doctor have both given explicit consent.
  • Photos, documents and live updates we share with the family during the visit.

2. Why we collect it

  • To prepare for and carry out hospital visits.
  • To keep the family informed and prepare visit summaries.
  • To arrange follow-ups, lab pickups, medicine delivery or second opinions you request.
  • To keep the senior safe, including in an emergency.
  • For billing and legal record-keeping.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use health information for advertising.

3. Consent

Before the first visit we collect written consent from the senior and authorization from the booking family member. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect visits already completed.

4. Who can see it

  • The family members you name during booking.
  • The companion assigned to the visit, and our supervising staff.
  • A licensed partner doctor, only when you request a second opinion.
  • Hospitals, labs or pharmacies, only as needed to carry out the visit.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

5. How we store and protect it

Records are kept in access-controlled, encrypted storage. Companions access only the information needed for their assigned visits. If a data breach occurs, we will notify affected families and the authorities as required by the DPDP Act.

6. How long we keep it

We keep visit records for as long as you use our service, and for up to 3 years after your last visit for continuity of care and legal purposes, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Families outside India

Our service is delivered in India and our records are held in India. If you live in the UK, EU or US, please note that information you share with us is processed under Indian law.
