Terms of Service

Last updated: 8 November 2026

These terms apply to services booked with Withyouseniors ("we", "us") by a family member ("you") for a senior ("the senior"). By booking a visit, you agree to these terms.

1. What our service is

We provide non-medical companionship and advocacy for hospital visits in India. This includes accompanying the senior, help with registration and queues, translation, asking prepared questions, taking notes, writing visit summaries and coordinating follow-ups.

2. No medical advice

Withyouseniors is not a healthcare provider. Our companions do not diagnose, give medical advice, or change, skip or suggest medicine doses. Visit summaries record what the treating doctor said. They are not medical opinions. Second opinions, when requested, are given by independent licensed doctors, who are responsible for their own advice.

3. Consent and decision-making

  • Written consent from the senior and authorization from the booking family member are required before the first visit.
  • At booking, you nominate one primary decision-maker (and optionally a backup). We follow only their instructions.
  • We never authorize admission, procedures or treatment on anyone's behalf.
  • Consultations are recorded only with the explicit consent of both the senior and the doctor.

4. Emergencies

In an emergency, the companion alerts hospital staff or emergency services right away and contacts the nominated decision-maker. We will act reasonably to keep the senior safe, but we are not an emergency medical service.

5. Payments

  • Our fees are agreed before the visit. Each visit includes a base number of hours, and extra time is billed per hour as agreed.
  • Hospital, lab, pharmacy and doctor bills are paid by your family directly to the provider. Our companions do not hold or pay these bills.
  • Companions never handle the senior's cash, ATM cards or personal documents unsupervised.

6. Cancellations and no-shows

  • You may cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the visit. Later cancellations may be charged.
  • If an assigned companion becomes unavailable, we will send a trained backup companion. If we cannot provide anyone, you will not be charged for that visit.

7. Hospital policies

Hospitals may restrict who enters consultation rooms or views records. We will make reasonable efforts but cannot guarantee access where a hospital refuses it.

8. Limitation of liability

We are responsible for providing our services with reasonable care and skill. We are not responsible for diagnoses, treatment, medical outcomes, or the acts of hospitals, doctors or other providers. To the extent permitted by law, our total liability for any visit is limited to the fees paid for that visit.

9. Privacy

Our handling of personal and health information is described in our Privacy Policy.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the courts of Bangalore, Karnataka.
